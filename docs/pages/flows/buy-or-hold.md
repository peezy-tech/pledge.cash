# Buy Or Hold Tokens

This flow is for token buyers, grant buyers, and holders. It is about reading protocol state before acting.

## 1. Identify The Project

Start from the Boardroom address and chain. Do not rely only on a ticker, social account, or screenshot.

Confirm:

- Boardroom address,
- share token address,
- owner,
- current status,
- official project links if available.

## 2. Identify The Offer

If you are buying from a sale or curve, inspect the distribution contract. If you are settling a paid grant, inspect the grant contract.

Check:

- payment token,
- price,
- available inventory or vested amount,
- timing,
- caps,
- status,
- recipient of funds or reserves.

## 3. Separate Token Rights From Project Claims

The token or grant right is on-chain. Marketing, social posts, advisory promises, investor updates, and legal terms are separate unless they are encoded in the contract.

Do not assume that a token gives you equity, profit share, legal governance, or employment rights.

## 4. Execute The Purchase Or Settlement

Use the app or contracts to approve any required payment token and complete the purchase or settlement.

After the transaction, verify the resulting token balance or grant state from the chain.

## 5. Monitor State

After buying or holding, watch:

- Boardroom status,
- treasury assets,
- grants and distributions,
- sale or curve status,
- locked liquidity,
- whether wind-down or redemptions have started.

## 6. Exit Or Redeem

If liquidity exists, you may be able to swap tokens in an AMM. If redemptions open, you may be able to burn shares for registered redeemable assets. These are different exit paths with different risks.
